# Enterprise Asset Lifecycle Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines general lifecycle guidance for
enterprise technology assets.

## Server Refresh

Production servers should normally be reviewed
after five years of service.

Assets with active critical security risks should
be prioritized for remediation or replacement.

Assets approaching end of vendor support should
be reviewed before the support expiration date.

## Warranty

Assets with expired warranty should be reviewed
for one of the following actions:

- Support renewal
- Replacement
- Retirement

The decision should consider business criticality,
security risk, operational condition, and cost.

## Security Risk

Assets with unresolved critical security risks
should receive priority review.

Replacement should not be automatically assumed
when remediation can reduce the risk.

## Approval

Replacement of production assets requires
appropriate business-owner approval.

High-impact asset actions must follow the
applicable approval and workflow process.

## Evidence Requirement

Recommendations must be based on available
asset records, risk information, cost information,
or approved enterprise policies.

If sufficient evidence is not available,
the system must report that additional
information is required.
